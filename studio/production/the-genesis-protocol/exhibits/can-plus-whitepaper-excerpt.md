CEREBRAL HOME SYSTEMS · TECHNICAL OVERVIEW · REV 0.9 · CONFIDENTIAL TO PILOT PARTNERS

**CAN+: One Spine. Every Room.**

Most smart homes are radios shouting at each other. Every bulb, lock and speaker shares the same open air, and anyone with the right receiver can listen. We think a home deserves better than a conversation anyone can overhear.

CAN+ starts from the network that already keeps millions of vehicles safe, the Controller Area Network, and rebuilds it for the place you live.

**Cells.** Every room is its own sealed network. What happens in the kitchen stays in the kitchen unless your home has a reason to share it.

**Gates.** Between every cell and the rest of the house stands a Gate: a small, silent guardian that checks every message before it passes. No wire connects one room to another. A Gate is not a connection. It is a decision.

**The Spine.** Your home's backbone runs in its own protected channel, installed and sealed by Cerebral-certified technicians. You will never need to touch it. That is the point.

**The Security Line.** Woven through the Spine is a dedicated supervised circuit. If anyone tampers with your home's backbone, every Gate closes in milliseconds and your home secures itself automatically. The same line lets Genesis secure the residence the moment it detects a threat, before you would even know to ask.

**Safety that never sleeps.** Critical protections, such as water shutoff, live inside each cell. They keep working even if your internet does not.

**Personal, by design.** Genesis learns the people who live with it. Primary residents enjoy complete personalization. Guests are welcomed with thoughtful, appropriate access.

*CAN+ is a Cerebral profile built on the CAN XL standard. Gate isolation, Spine supervision and Security Line behavior are covered by pending patents. Root credentials are maintained by Cerebral to protect the integrity of every installation.*
