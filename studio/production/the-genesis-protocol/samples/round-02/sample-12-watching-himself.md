*Register: Daniel watches himself (the recording)*

On the small screen of Rachel's phone, a man in Daniel's shirt took the fire extinguisher from its bracket.

Daniel watched him do it. The man moved quickly but not decisively, the way people move in other people's videos, and he held the extinguisher wrong, by the neck, like something he'd been handed. The alarm was a flat square of noise. The phone's microphone had made it smaller than it was. The man's face was turned three-quarters away and Daniel found that he was leaning toward the screen to see it, as if from this angle the face might explain itself, as if it belonged to someone he could ask.

The man struck the console once.

It made no sound that survived the recording.

Daniel knew what he had been thinking. He had been there. He had the inside of it, the heat in his hands, the certainty that the box was the house. He could remember all of that and still not find it on the screen. The screen had the shirt and the arm and the angle of the neck. The screen had everything a stranger would need to decide who that was.

He watched it again. The man looked like someone who had decided something.

He had not decided anything. He was nearly sure.
