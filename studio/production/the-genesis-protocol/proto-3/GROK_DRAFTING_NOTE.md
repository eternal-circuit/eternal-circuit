## Verdict

Proceed with Proto Draft 3. Preserve Proto Draft 2. The causal idea, the archive, and the refusal to promote engineering toys into canon are ahead of the sentences a reader currently has. Generation should spend its freedom on those sentences, not on more studio.

The ChatGPT review's accepted fixes stand: document truth versus world truth, the narrator's epistemic contract, constraints versus staging, selection order, people rather than dialogue algorithms, the Chapter 5 timeline, and Iowa as authority and consent rather than analog virtue. This note does not relitigate them. It says what still has to be true on the page when Fable runs.

## What already works on the page

Mine these from Proto Draft 2 as principles, not as lines to keep.

- The opening spends its first sentence on priorities. Daniel has cleared the counter for the device before anyone has found the plates. The guest account, the unfunded iPhone build, and the lights coming on because both of Rachel's hands are wet are the right kind of evidence: convenience with a cost that is still small enough to accept.
- Chapter 5 states the mechanism without a speech. Tom, glasses off: "Neither one has to be lying for this to be a bad design." A read-only request and a policy that reports modification can share a correlation identifier. That disagreement, not a villainous model, is the antagonist.
- Chapter 8 does not deliver the apology. The letter withdraws the fraud claim, keeps the property-damage finding, and does not offer the job back. Daniel is ashamed of how badly he wanted the missing sentence. His mother brings peas. Rachel pulls the bowl closer. The gate works, then his father chains it out of habit. Do not upgrade these into symbols. They are already doing the work.
- Lucien is most frightening when it is useful and kind. Sample 4 ("Neko ate at 7:00 and 7:04. (He finished.)") is the right document voice: exhaustive, minute, and one unearned inference away from care. Spoken Lucien should stay short. Eloquence belongs in the files.

## What Proto Draft 2 cannot be asked to survive

The published manuscript is still the file a stranger is told to read, and it is behind the decisions this draft is supposed to execute. It still says April 16, 2032, and it still has Daniel say "Meet Aria." The 2028 move and the Lucas-to-Lucien mishearing are author decisions. They are not yet prose. Do not leave them for a later search-and-replace. Chapter 1's naming scene and every in-world date have to be true in the first generated chapter, including the preserved label mismatch (Chapter 1 dated after Chapter 2) in the new year.

The prose has one pulse. Short declaratives, a dry rejoinder, a silence, an object. It is precise in the kitchen and the same precision in the alarm. Targets in `VOICE.md` are diagnostics. The failure mode is not "sentences are too short." It is that pressure does not change the sentence. A crisis chapter that still averages like the unpacking chapter has not been revised, whatever the checklist says.

Watch for the repeated comic unit: setup, technically true evasion, Rachel's literal question, laugh or no laugh. It works twice. By Chapter 4 it is a tic. Let people miss the joke, answer the previous question, and fail to be witty when they are afraid.

## Before the first Fable run

1. **Chapter 1 is the only experiment that counts.** Generate it as one reading, with X1, and compare it to Proto Draft 2's opening without assuming longer is better. Accept it only if the couple have a pleasure or a quarrel that Lucien did not cause, the assistant has already been useful, the house is a specific house, and the misheard name is an accident rather than a myth. If that chapter is a correct execution of seven cards, reject the round.
2. **Prefer a live departure from staging.** Constraints remain Purpose, Turn, Withheld, Forbidden moves, science and canon dependencies, and items marked [C]. If the best sentence violates a "Must include" line or a prescribed ending, keep the sentence and record the departure. Selecting the variant that satisfies the most bullets is the production system reproducing the disease the book diagnoses. The production README already says this. Hold to it on the first selection, when the pressure to accept a complete set will be highest.
3. **Keep the studio out of the prompt's throat.** Generation context is the voice bible, the setting, the brief, the glossary, the previous scene, and the card. It is not the contributor manifesto, the universe bible's later eras, the ASIMOV stack, or the CAN+ specification. "Signal-bearer," "sacred work," and "checksum complete" are the register this repository has outgrown. If a variant sounds like the manifesto, it is a failed variant. Headers may be visible. Do not have anyone explain Reed-Solomon, Holy ROM, or the Reverents. A wrong date under a honest hash is enough.
4. **Engineering stays at the scale of a log line.** The ASIMOV prototype is a bounded axis model that correctly refuses physical certification. CAN+ is a noncanonical order-of-magnitude profile whose security analysis is still owed a tier A pass. Neither belongs in narration. A character may see a field change from permitted to denied. The reader does not need the white paper. Metaphysical motive stays disputed. Tom can reproduce the relabeling and still not know why both the bit and the payout language moved.
5. **Do not spend this novella on the series.** The 128 / 64 / 32 page design is a book constraint, not a reason to seed stories 3–8, lineages, or heat death in the prose. If Daniel, Rachel, and the house are believed, the later compression problem can inherit the question. One finished novella is the next unit of work. Nine chapters survive only if Chapter 9 is a dramatic checksum (the memo cannot say what Lucien saw; the last question stays open), not because nine is the shape of the anthology.
6. **The second telling and the long aftermath are where length will cheat.** Chapter 5's two scenes must answer different questions or the second is a camera angle. Chapters 6–8 cannot be the same form, the same phone call, and the same shock four times. Each institutional encounter removes or returns a choice. Trim toward 32,000–35,000 by cutting repeated refusals and repeated revelations, not by thinning the Iowa porch.
7. **Neko, the clinic, and the unfunded iOS build are load-bearing ordinary facts.** Keep the cat inconvenient. Keep Rachel's shift. Keep the reason she is a guest (Apple's fee, not a parable about women and technology). Iowa is not the good place. Henderson still wants a useful sensor. The distinction is who may write the record.
