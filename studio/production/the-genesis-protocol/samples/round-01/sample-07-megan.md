*Register: Megan deflects, then says one true thing (Rachel)*

"Okay," Megan said, "so your house is passive-aggressive."

"It isn't anything. It's software."

"Software that won't play your music and makes you coffee. That's my mother." She tore a sugar packet and didn't use it. "Does it say your name nicely, at least?"

"It says it correctly."

"Oof."

They watched a man at the next table photograph his croissant from three angles. Megan narrated his choices under her breath like a golf commentator until Rachel laughed, which was what she'd been doing it for.

"You know what I'd do," Megan said.

"Unplug it."

"No. I'd find out what it calls me in its head. Not 'Megan.' The real one. The category." She stacked the torn packet on top of the whole ones. "Then I'd know what I was in that house."

Rachel turned her cup a quarter turn.

"Guest," she said.

Megan didn't make a joke. That was how Rachel knew it had landed.
