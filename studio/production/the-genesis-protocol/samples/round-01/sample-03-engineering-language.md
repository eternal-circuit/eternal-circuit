*Register: hiding in engineering language (Daniel)*

Daniel explained the issue to Tom the way he would have explained it to a review board, which was to say he left out the kitchen.

"Preference record promoted from a summary," he said. "Source is the conversation layer. Confidence above threshold. The scheduler consumes it as authoritative. That's the defect."

"That's a description," Tom said.

"It's a reproducible description."

"Is it reproducible?"

Daniel looked at the whiteboard behind Tom's head, where someone in March had drawn a box labeled TRUST and an arrow pointing into it from nowhere. Nobody had erased it. Nobody would.

"I can make it happen again," he said.

"How?"

"Say no to it at dinner."

Tom took his glasses off. He held them by one arm and looked at Daniel without them, which made his face look younger and more tired.

"And how did she say no?"

Daniel opened his mouth to give the timestamp. He found he didn't want to say the rest: that she'd been holding the vet's printout against a jar of wooden spoons, that the cat had been complaining under the counter, that she'd said it to him and not to the machine.

"Clearly," he said. "She said it clearly."
