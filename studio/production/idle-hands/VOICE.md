# Idle Hands — voice bible

**Status:** DRAFT, 2026-11-24. Seed and register **author-confirmed**: Kurt Vonnegut, *Player Piano*; warm, sad satire. Other sections are proposals. · **Register ID:** `0b00000001` · **Budget:** 64 pages, about 16,000–17,500 words · **Setting:** around 2035, after ASIMOV · Background: [SEED_RESEARCH.md](SEED_RESEARCH.md)

## The feeling to leave

Warm, sad satire. The reader should laugh at the institutions, love the people, and close the story knowing that nothing went wrong and a young man lost his future anyway. *Genesis Protocol* asked whether the machine was lying. *Idle Hands* shows the machine working perfectly.

## Record type

Proposed: a labor and training record. The apprentice's story arrives through the register alongside placement-center files, shift tickets and ASIMOV-signed teleoperation receipts. The closing ticket, *Resolved locally.*, is itself a register entry.

## Point of view, person, tense, distance

Proposed: third person, past tense, close to the apprentice, with plain narration that occasionally steps back to describe an institution the way a patient, slightly amused observer would. No first person and no author intruding, which follows the seed's early mode.

## Reliability

The prose is reliable. The *documents* are cheerful. Every institutional record in the story is accurate and upbeat: a successful placement, a credited contribution, a resolved ticket. The satire lives in the gap between the true record and what it costs.

## From the seed (borrow)

- **Plain sentences carrying heavy facts.** State the devastating thing simply and move on.
- **Irony and sentimentality together.** Mock the system; never mock the people inside it.
- **Satire aimed at institutions.** Success metrics, training stipends, partnership language, wellness automation, a placement that counts as a success while the queue dries up.
- **The recorded craftsman.** The apprentice's best repair becomes the fleet's demonstration. The structure is the homage; no scene copies the seed.
- **Short chapters and quick cuts** between the plant, the training center and home.
- **Decent people in an indecent arrangement.** The veteran, the coordinator and the fleet lead each mean well and help build the outcome.

## From the seed (do not borrow)

- Ilium, the Reeks and Wrecks, Rudy Hertz, Paul Proteus, or any invented place, term or character.
- Signature refrains or catchphrases from any Vonnegut book, and his later metafiction or author intrusions.
- A literal player piano. **Open:** one deliberate allusion (for example, an object in a bar or church) may be approved by the author and must be recorded on its scene card.
- A revolt plot. *Player Piano* builds toward an uprising; *Idle Hands* ends quietly.

## Rhythm and diction

- Short declaratives, many under twelve words, with an occasional longer sentence for a joke's setup.
- Concrete trade vocabulary for maintenance: brackets, sensors, torque, load, trips. Institutional vocabulary rendered deadpan: *placement*, *pathway*, *partner*, *engagement*.
- Avoid sneering, cynicism, sermons, stock phrases, and any sentence that states the theme.

## Dialogue

- **The apprentice:** eager, polite, asks practical questions.
- **The veteran technician:** dry, generous, explains by doing.
- **The placement coordinator:** warm, sincere, fluent in metrics. She believes every word.
- **The fleet lead:** kind, credits him by name, has no hours to give.
- **The mother:** few words, all of them practical. She sets food aside for whoever arrives after closing.
- **The friendly rival:** funny, gets the offer.
- **ASIMOV and the fleet:** short, correct, courteous notifications. Never menacing.

## Motifs and objects

The bracket that flexes; the first crate reaching the truck and the wave from the loading dock; the stipend tied to attendance; the pantry van; the food set aside after closing; the teleoperation headset; the shift queue; the ticket.

## Corruption level

Low, slightly above *Genesis Protocol*. Proposed: register metadata only. For example, a session receipt's operator field may be normalized to a fleet ID in one entry, which is ambiguous between privacy redaction and the record forgetting who did the work.

## Theological layer

Physically, the fleet learns a demonstrated skill. Socially, institutions count him as a success. Metaphysically, the proverb in the title: what becomes of a person whose hands are no longer needed? Leave it open. The pantry van, work done for others that no market pays for, is the story's quiet answer, never stated.

## Structure

Proposed: 25–30 short scenes averaging about 600 words, in 6–8 chapters. To be built as scene cards once the outline is confirmed.
