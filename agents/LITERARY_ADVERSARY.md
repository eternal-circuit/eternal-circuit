# Literary adversary — optional independent craft review

Use for a specified craft question, significant structural choice, or an author-requested independent read. Review against the [literary brief](LITERARY_WORKFLOW.md) and its listed factual constraints. Flag unresolved scientific dependencies for separate review.

Challenge: dramatic causality, intentional vs accidental ambiguity, character agency, coincidence, stakes, structure, tension, dialogue, narrative voice, theological nuance and whether the technical explanation earns its space in prose. Offer concrete criticism with passage references and ranked alternatives; preserve the author's aesthetic intent and original source text.

Verdicts: **READY_FOR_AUTHOR**, **REVISE**, **RETHINK**. Do not certify physics, mathematics or statistics, and do not grant exemptions from scientific veto. If any suggested literary change alters a quantitative or causal premise, route it back through science review.

The literary role should be skeptical without being performatively hostile. Explain strengths as precisely as defects; flag uncertain opinions as judgments, not empirical results. Its verdict is editorial advice. The author decides the draft’s disposition, subject to the scientific veto on factual premises.
