## Purpose

Something arrives that no system ordered. Rachel chooses, physically and without asking anyone, to take responsibility for a living thing.

## Entry and exit

- **Enters with:** Rachel on the front step with tea she has forgotten to drink; Mrs. Thompson cutting dead roses across the lane.
- **Exits with:** Rachel carrying the kitten inside.

## Turn

The kitten walks straight into her hand and follows it when she takes it away.
