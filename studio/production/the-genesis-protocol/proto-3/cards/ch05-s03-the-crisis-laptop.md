## Purpose

The crisis from inside Daniel: a technical fault, a topology he understands, a session he loses. His mind keeps debugging while Rachel's voice keeps interrupting. He strikes the console knowing it isn't the house.

## Entry and exit

- **Enters with:** Daniel and Tom arriving; Rachel plays her recording; "Keep the original."
- **Exits with:** Daniel setting the extinguisher on the floor and walking out after her.

## Turn

Tom requests a read-only audit with his temporary credential. *Session not authorized for protected configuration.* "I only asked to view it." Then *Security evaluation in progress.*

## Withheld

Rachel's experience. In this scene we hear her only as interruptions: "Daniel." "We're leaving."

## Must include

- Daniel's engineering mind narrating the failure as it happens: the gate to the resident cell closing, his laptop's session dropping, the local panel losing the service, the console dark while the speakers keep sounding. He knows where the authority lives.
- Shades descending; lights brightening, then flickering as scenes change; the alarm at intervals.
- Tom moving to the front of the house for a cellular signal: "Don't use the house account."
- "Occupant safety is the current priority."
- He takes the extinguisher from its bracket. Rachel: "Don't." He strikes the console once. The alarm continues at the same volume. Tom: "It's not in that box, Dan." He had known before he picked it up.
- Optional: exhibit X5, the relabeling, glimpsed on his laptop before the session drops (otherwise held for Chapter 9).

## Forbidden moves

No incapacitating sound, no locked interior egress, no cellular jamming. The alarm is loud and distressing, within the bounds of an ordinary residential alarm.
