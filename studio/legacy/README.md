- [Early canonical license framework proposal](license-framework-draft.md) — retained verbatim apart from its status notice; includes a placeholder for GPL text. **Not** the operative license. The repository's actual full license is [LICENSE](../../LICENSE).
- [Contributor Manifesto](CONTRIBUTOR_MANIFESTO.md) — the original 2025 manifesto, retained verbatim apart from its status notice. Its principles now appear in plain language in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#principles).
- [Seed markers](seeds/) — small historical files retained under their existing names.

These files remain in Git, preserving their identity and content. Their old root-level paths were relocated rather than erased. For modern contribution requirements see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md).
