## Purpose

Show Cerebral from inside, and introduce Tom before he becomes important. Establish what Daniel owns (the part that connects things) and what he doesn't (the models, the policy, the partner).

## Entry and exit

- **Enters with:** Daniel arrives early for a meeting that has already been moved again.
- **Exits with:** Daniel texting Rachel about dinner ("Somewhere with plates"), confident about a release he only partly understands.

## Turn

On his service-cell credential Daniel opens the dashboard for his own house: cells, gates, green status lights. He can see all of it and change almost none of it. He finds this reassuring.

## Withheld

That the managed policy service belongs to another team and a partner. At most, one passing mention of Envision Dynamics on a slide he skims.

## Must include

- **Tom**, introduced warmly: dry, precise, helped them move the mattress with a hand truck, asks about the house. Their friendship has to be real.
- Bob is **never heard directly**. The meeting happens behind a door or on a calendar. Bob's sayings arrive secondhand through Tom or Daniel's memory.
- The leak-shutoff valve: Daniel spent a month arguing it belonged in the trial. He's proud of it.
- Rachel's texts: "Missed you this morning. Thanks for the coffee." / "Sorry. Bob moved the meeting. Dinner tonight? Somewhere with plates." / "Yes. But I'm choosing." / "Deal."

## Forbidden moves

No villainous executives. No exposition dump about CAN+; at most one line about cells and gates on the dashboard.

- v2: centered on the dashboard
- v3: centered on the rescheduled meeting and the waiting
