# Orchestrator runbook

Read [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), the [project inventory](../canon/PROJECT_INVENTORY.md), the relevant canon, source notes and manuscript passages. Record the requested deliverable and publication classification. Choose the route by the decision the work needs to support.

## Universe and scientific canon

Use this route for substantial setting work such as dates and dependencies, technological capability, deployment and interoperability, labor and other economic outcomes, and demographic or physical limits. An ASIMOV specification and the dates at which its components become viable belong here.

1. **Scope with a literary canon check.** Have the Canon Keeper identify established dates, places, events and outcomes that make the question relevant. Distinguish confirmed canon from drafts, disputed records and open choices. Use this context to narrow the first research pass, especially when theoretical possibilities are broad. Separate physical feasibility, implementation, adoption and downstream effects. If evidence leads outside the starting scope or challenges a canonical premise, preserve the finding and identify the affected canon and possible revisions for author review. The Scientific Adversary determines the verdict from evidence and argument.
2. **Research.** Run the Frontier Scout, Evidence Verifier, Quantitative Modeler, and Canon Keeper in parallel where feasible. Use original sources, report what was measured, and quantify relevant budgets and sensitivity. Record the assumptions behind each timeline or economic inference.
3. **Deposit.** Save a self-contained research note under `studio/research/notes/YYYY/` using the [template](../research/TEMPLATE.md). Register it in the [index](../research/index.md). Have a verifier check it and pin the revision in the [claim dossier](../research/reviews/TEMPLATE.md).
4. **Falsify.** Give the evidence and model to an independent Scientific Adversary. Record PASS, CONDITIONAL PASS, BLOCK or UNRESOLVED per claim, with reasons, scope, dissent and triggers for re-review. Missing review leaves the claim unresolved.
5. **Decide and archive.** For background canon, a science PASS on every indispensable claim goes to the author with a reviewable diff. The archivist records sources, verdicts, decisions and rejected variants. A research PR can preserve unresolved findings as research. Canon promotion requires explicit author approval.
6. **Return findings.** If a literary check initiated the task, return a verdict for its exact claim, the applicable era and domain, assumptions, observable consequences and pinned sources. Give the writer a cleared alternative or the remaining open question when appropriate. Identify any proposed canon changes awaiting author approval.

## Literary work

Use the [literary workflow](LITERARY_WORKFLOW.md) for prose, outlines and scene revision. Default to an author-led drafting loop. Bring in a separate craft reader for a specific question when warranted. Verify consequential claims against the scientific canon during the continuity pass. Open a universe task immediately for any consequential dependency that remains unverified. Scope the task to the specific claim so other drafting can continue.

**Example.** Model whether a warehouse can reduce staffing through the universe workflow. Once the setting conditions are documented, draft how the apprentice learns the news in the literary workflow. If the draft turns a reversible staffing trial into permanent fleet-wide cuts, refer that changed economic claim.

## Shared completion rules

- Use a named branch and reviewable PR, with the proposed changes shown as a diff.
- Distinguish research publication, an editorial draft and canon promotion. Record author approval for promotion.
- Preserve existing prose unless the author has requested an edit. Resolve uncertainty about publication rights before uploading private material.
- Report blockers at the level of the affected claim or deliverable and record what would resolve them.
