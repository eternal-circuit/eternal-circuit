## Purpose

The harassment reaches the house before they do. Rachel goes back one last time with the property manager. Grief arrives inside an ordinary task (target: voice sample 6). She decides they're leaving for Iowa.

## Entry and exit

- **Enters with:** the property manager's photographs of unwanted food deliveries at the gate; their phone numbers in posts telling people to "demand the truth"; Rachel no longer answering unfamiliar calls.
- **Exits with:** "We're going to Iowa for a while." / "That far?" / "I think so."

## Turn

On the kitchen floor with the cartoon-tooth mug in her lap, she understands that what she's losing isn't the house but the week she would have had.
