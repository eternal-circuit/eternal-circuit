## Purpose

Open the chapter in Daniel's head for the first time. He loves her, and he leaves anyway. He delegates the tenderness: he makes the coffee and asks the house to keep it warm and to tell her.

## Entry and exit

- **Enters with:** Bob has moved the meeting to early morning. Rachel is asleep.
- **Exits with:** Daniel on I-280 in the dark, pleased with himself for the coffee and already thinking about the release.

## Turn

He writes "Made this before I left" on the back of a packing label and asks Aria to keep the mug warm and notify Rachel when she comes down. He thinks this counts as being there.

## Withheld

That the note and the warm coffee are a substitute for him. He doesn't see it; the reader should.

## Must include

- The note on the back of a packing label, adhesive on one corner.
- The device is now Lucien. Daniel says the name without a flicker (or with one, consistent with the chosen Chapter 1 naming variant).
- He dresses in the dark; the second monitor on the floor by the mattress.
- One detail from the setting bible on the drive down (the lane, the eucalyptus, I-280's lights).

## Forbidden moves

No self-aware guilt. No narration about technology replacing love.
