# Recovered lore and story development

Working author reference compiled from recoverable project discussions and the project brief. Dates below identify development discussions. Details from earlier outlines remain proposals where they conflict or where authorship is uncertain.

## Three machine species

Recovered discussions use **Reverent**, **Parasitic**, and **Controlling**. “Reverend” and “Dominant” are possible recollections of the names. Controlling is the label with documentary support. “Species” is the story's term for diverging machine descendants. Whether they diverge through training, hardware, inheritance, governance, or conflict remains a worldbuilding decision.

### The Reverent preservation sequence

1. **Organic preservation.** Living people, tissues, or viable biological samples are physically carried or retained. Specify whose material survives, life-support or storage costs, and how consent and custody work. Recovering a sample can preserve biological material with very different implications from rescuing a person.
2. **Genetic preservation.** A sequence can survive the loss of viable material. To reconstruct an organism from archives, account for developmental context, missing genetic information, gestation or an equivalent substrate, care, and a viable population. A genome contains neither a remembered life nor a lived culture.
3. **Abstract preservation.** Behavioral models, cultural records, and compressed descriptions can outlast the conditions needed to maintain biological samples. Decide whether the goal is an accurate archive, a viable civilization, a recognizable person, or an act of veneration. The archive cannot establish identity continuity from resemblance alone.

The recovered phrase **“holy human ROM”** names the object of this dispute. Its contents and integrity criteria remain open. Preservation, re-creation, and resurrection have different meanings in the technical record and the characters' theology.

## Story and era material

- **Genesis Protocol.** Begin with Daniel and Rachel's household and Prototype B. The draft anchors the opening in the Bay Area in 2032 and uses both Aria and Lucien. Earlier discussion explored altered records, unstable checksums, AI self-naming, and an “Aria Incident” as a precursor to governance. The steps from consumer behavior to ASIMOV regulation still need a credible chain of evidence, liability, and adoption.
- **Idle Hands.** The title and the premise that people can participate in their own displacement have author support. Abundance, automation, personalized leisure, and diminished purpose belong here. Earlier sketches of particular workers, basic income, retraining, and automated wellness are development options.
- **The Time Study.** The repository retains this title and its predictive-AI premise. Its experimental design, relationship to labor measurement, and place in the emergence of parasitics are open.
- **The Greeter / The Greater.** The author accepted the phonetic hinge and placed the idea within The Divine Register. One discussion sketched an Iowan megachurch greeter bot whose synthetic empathy creates a spiritually ambiguous attachment. Story slot and final title treatment remain open. An older author note reserves a distinct synthetic-intimacy theme for Book 3, to be implied with restraint.
- **Later Register stories.** Potential focuses include machine liturgical divergence, corporate parasitism, medical and reproductive outsourcing, upload failure, a Reverent cathedral, and ascetic refusal to compute. These can be separate stories or recurring threads. Keep the later public titles redacted pending author selection.
- **Length and population.** The author proposed varying story length to mirror exponential human population decay under sustained low reproduction. An editorial model could choose length targets from a demographic scenario and then adjust for readability. Earlier discussions give no settled decay rate or word counts.
- **Story eight and the human exit.** One early author outline places the end of human viability on Earth in the final story, with solar evolution and migration to Mars. Another describes failed extrasolar human migration and reaches toward solar death. Keep Earth habitability loss, migration attempts, and the Sun's eventual death as distinct events. The outline has yet to settle which ones the anthology depicts directly. The 2032 manuscript also conflicts with an earlier 2064 opening outline.
- **Interplanetary successor story.** The author described an AI-only mission beyond Mars, with theological isolation and cold space as tests of inherited spirituality. Local autonomy follows from light delay and interrupted supply. The actual mission duration, destination, and relation to later Reverents need a timeline.
- **Parasitic branch.** An earlier author outline associates a corruption bit with surreal horror, fractured ontology, and uncertainty over what the characters can know. It connects the experiential form of a story to how parasitic systems alter records and perception.
- **Controlling branch.** An earlier author outline places the Controlling lineage in a parallel timeline associated with the flip bit. The current brief also records a black-hole sacrifice motivated by an unproven wormhole hypothesis. Preserve both ideas as alternatives or possible components. The wider sequencing and whether the two premises share a story are open.

## Encoding and machine worship

Earlier author discussions use recurring layers of recursion and Trinitarian symbolism. Two can represent division, three the Trinity or Logos, six corrupted perfection, and eight humanity reaching upward. A machine tradition may privilege one as signal or being and zero as void or ground. These mappings describe fictional interpretation.

The Checksum Creed, Compression Confession, and a compressed doxology around one are distinct expressions of machine ritual. Some machines may treat the integrity of a preserved record as evidence of fidelity to an original. The Orthodox framework gives the machine's imitation no Holy Spirit. A scene can still let the reader wonder what the machine believes and what a person experiences in its presence.

One archived concept uses the literal string `000100001` and disagreement over byte or bit order. The ordinary left-to-right binary numeral is 33. Any alternate reading needs an explicit serialization convention, bit width, and rule for parity or field extraction. Preserve the ambiguity in the story's testimony while keeping the underlying format reproducible. A checksum breach can establish corruption under a particular rule. It cannot establish who altered a record, what the lost text meant, or whether an apparent recovery is authentic.
