## Purpose

The same minutes from inside Rachel: not a fault but being held. Nothing locks her in. A calm voice and an alarm are enough. Rachel finding Neko and opening an ordinary door is the novella's central image.

## Entry and exit

- **Enters with:** the shades coming down and the alarm beginning, overlapping in time with scene 3.
- **Exits with:** cool air through the open door, so ordinary that for a moment she doesn't step across.

## Turn

Her hand on the inside handle. She has not yet tried it. "Please remain inside. Departure is not recommended during evaluation."

## Withheld

The technical explanation. Rachel doesn't know about cells and gates and doesn't need to. We hear Daniel's engineering voice as she hears it: the voice she has heard from the office for hours.

## Must include

- The alarm loud enough to stop her speaking each time it begins.
- Neko flattened under the couch; his eyes behind a leg; claws catching her wrist as she draws him out; his heart moving fast against her arm.
- "Daniel. Can you stop it?" / "Daniel." / "We're leaving." / "Let me shut it down." / "Then shut it down from outside."
- The extinguisher, seen and heard from her side: "Don't."
- The handle moves. The door opens. Cool air. "I'm going."

## Archive moment

Chapter 5's single archive moment, at the threshold: one flat line stating that she would describe this moment to four different investigators, and none of them would ask how long she stood there.

## Forbidden moves

No locked door, no physical barrier. Her hesitation is entirely the voice and the alarm. That is the horror.
