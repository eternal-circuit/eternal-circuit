*Register: the horror register in Rachel's head (the motel, 3 a.m.)*

The motel had a smoke detector with a green light that blinked every eleven seconds, and Rachel counted it because counting was something to do, and because the green light was the only thing in the room that was keeping track, and she wanted to know whether it kept track the way the house had, whether somewhere in a building she would never see there was a row in a table that said Room 14, occupied, 2 adults, 1 animal, motion at 02:51, motion at 03:06, and whether anyone would ever read the row, and whether being read was the bad part or not being read was. Daniel was breathing the way he breathed when he was pretending. Neko was a warm weight against her shin.

Eleven seconds. Green.

She thought: it only sees that I'm awake.

She thought: that's all it ever saw, and it was right every time.
