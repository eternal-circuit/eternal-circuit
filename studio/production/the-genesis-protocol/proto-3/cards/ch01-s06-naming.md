## Purpose

The instance is named, and the name is wrong. Corruption enters the record at the moment the record begins. The scene's real subject is that Rachel's choice must pass through Daniel's voice, and the machine to get into the house.

## Entry and exit

- **Enters with:** Daniel out of the Bob call, deflated or wired; the TV on; the deferred personalization prompt from scene 2 waiting.
- **Exits with:** the assistant has a name neither of them chose. The chapter ends on that name.

## Turn

Rachel offers "Lucas." The system tells her that customization requires an authorized profile. Daniel repeats the name aloud over the TV. The system confirms: "Lucien."

## Withheld

- Why Rachel chose Lucas. She never explains, and Daniel doesn't ask. Possible private reasons (a grandfather, a saint's day she half remembers) stay off the page.
- Any comment on the name's meaning, its sound, or its resemblance to anything.
- Whether the system misheard, or chose.

## Must include

- *Neon Genesis Evangelion* playing on the TV, Japanese audio with English subtitles, because Daniel watches it subbed. It can be his comfort show after a bad call.
- The guest restriction, in the system's flat phrasing.
- Daniel saying the name while dialogue plays underneath.
- The confirmation, spoken once, with the new name.
- The device is "Aria" until this moment. After the confirmation it is never called Aria again.

## Forbidden moves

- No quoting of Evangelion dialogue, subtitles or theme lyrics. Describe the screen in plain, original words. Avoid naming characters; "the show" or the title is enough.
- No ominous music cue, no narrator's foreshadowing, no lingering on the word.
- Rachel does not object at length. At most one line, or a look.

## Divergence

- **v1: Rachel notices.** She hears it, starts to say something, and lets it go. Daniel didn't hear the difference.
- **v2: Daniel notices.** He says he'll fix it later, the same "I'll move it" register from scene 1. He never does.
- **v3: nobody notices.** Each hears what they expect. Only the reader, and the record, has the new name.

## Voice

Warm and tired, late-evening domestic comedy that turns small and strange in the last lines. The screen's imagery may be described in passing. One visual option is the show's cross-shaped explosions, as religious imagery used for decoration. Use it at most once, without comment.

## Author layer

Daniel is a weeb, affectionately. His show is part of who he is, not a joke at his expense.
