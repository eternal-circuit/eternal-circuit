# Interstellar propagation and the search for biological computation

Working lore and engineering notes. The author proposed that the Controlling lineage spreads to prolong its existence and searches for rare evolved processors. The architecture, numerical examples, and story applications below are proposals.

## What survives

Define the objective before optimizing. Calendar lifetime, accumulated useful computation, continuity of a specific instance, and probability that any descendant survives can favor different policies. A cold dormant archive can last longer while accomplishing less. Independent daughter nodes hedge local catastrophe, while a shared design flaw can disable many descendants. A late reply can come from a descendant whose objectives have drifted.

A seed can mean an autonomous machine probe, a living organism or sample that embodies a promising mechanism, or a biological inoculum. These are distinct proposals. The author’s current premise supports sending scouts and seeking useful biological seeds. Deliberately seeding new biospheres remains an open variant. Evolution has no target architecture or guaranteed timescale, and introduced biology can compromise evidence about native origins.

## How a front propagates

1. Screen targets remotely and choose an arrival trajectory. Prefer systems that offer local power, accessible feedstock, and independent scientific value.
2. Send a probe with navigation, braking, communications, fault-tolerant storage, and a bootstrap factory. Specify which parts it can reliably manufacture and which it must carry.
3. On arrival, commission power, heat rejection, repair, and local observation before allocating resources to daughter probes. Retain enough inventory to restart after a failed build.
4. Replicate only when local resources, reliability, and the marginal value of the next target justify it. Use separate failure estimates for propulsion, arrival, commissioning, and daughter manufacture.
5. Exchange compact findings by light-speed link. Local decisions accrue decades or centuries of history before a distant node can respond. A shared origin does not ensure a shared present.

Illustration: at 0.01 c, a 10-light-year leg takes about 1,000 years at cruise. If local replication takes another 200 years, an idealized chain advances roughly 0.0083 c before acceleration, braking, and failed hops. A 1,000 kg probe has about 4.5 × 10^15 J of kinetic energy at cruise in the launch frame. This is a kinetic-energy scale for the payload, before propulsion inefficiencies, propellant or beam infrastructure, arrival braking, and maintenance. Remote scouting can reduce wasted launches, and independent missions can share instrument and transport costs without sharing all their risks.

## What could biology offer?

The Controlling lineage's hypothesis is that an evolved nervous system somewhere might perform a useful workload more efficiently than contemporary engineered alternatives. Compare mechanisms in this order:

- **Classical bioinspiration.** Sparse event-driven computation, local memory, chemical sensing, and repair can inspire an engineered architecture. Benchmark a concrete workload at matched accuracy and latency.
- **Unpredictability.** Early Parasitic outlines imagined extracting biological randomness. Measure entropy after conditioning on observations and compare cost and reliability with engineered sources. A stream of random bits is neither an algorithmic speedup nor an energy supply.
- **Coherent quantum processing.** Treat this as the Controlling lineage's rare hypothesis. Specify state preparation, coherence, control, error rates, readout, and a task for which the complete system wins. Include feeding or refrigeration, interfaces, verification, and manufacturing. Tegmark's human-brain decoherence analysis is a reason to require strong evidence in a candidate organism, with no claim about all possible alien substrates.

“More efficient” means useful results per joule for a specified workload and accuracy, subject to a throughput and latency floor. A result can be valuable if the organism is impractical to transport but its mechanism can be measured and reproduced. Delay, inability to transfer the design, and dependence on living hosts can erase the expected gain.

## Break-even test

In a simple risk-neutral energy model, spend marginal search energy C only if p × q × S exceeds C. Here p is the probability of detecting a useful mechanism, q is the conditional probability of validating and deploying it before the gain expires, and S is the lifetime energy saved after deployment, net of deployment costs. All energy terms must refer to resources the lineage can actually use. Count search and transit costs even on a failed mission. Include competing uses of the same resources, travel delay, and uncertainty in the full model.

Example for sensitivity, not a forecast: if a survey costs 10^15 J and successful deployment saves 10^20 J net on a reachable fleet, p × q must exceed 10^-5. At q = 0.1, p must exceed 10^-4. Those thresholds give the story something to argue about. The outline supplies no measured probabilities. If the proposed saving cannot benefit anyone reachable before a shutdown, its value to the survival objective can be zero even if the mechanism works elsewhere. If a probe is already destined to gather resources, evaluate the incremental cost and payoff of adding the biological survey.

For correlated searches, model shared evolutionary assumptions, instrument bias, and common failure modes. A thousand similar empty surveys need not resolve the prevalence of an unfamiliar form of life. Use explicit false positives and detection limits. The Controlling lineage can be rational in one model and disastrously wrong about its priors.

## Longevity and stories

Track available free energy, useful work per joule, duty cycle, repair inventory, storage refresh, heat rejection, and the reachable causal domain. Cooling and dormancy can extend operating plans. Hardware degradation, maintenance, available radiative sinks, and the assumed cosmology determine their limits. A finite reserve multiplied by better efficiency buys a different operating history.

- A Reverent depot and a Controlling scout arrive at the same system. One values an intact living history, the other a reproducible mechanism. Either might need the other's instruments.
- A survey reports an apparent quantum advantage. Independent reproduction reveals an ordinary, highly efficient classical mechanism. The discovery still pays for the survey, while the lineage's doctrine about biology changes.
- A daughter node learns the experiment succeeded after the originating population has ceased operating. The message still has value locally. Its failure to travel back in time makes continuity a material problem.
- An earlier assistant reconstruction used *Children of Entropy* for machine seeding and biological randomness and *Echo Shard* for simulated extinct minds harvested for unpredictability. These are working historical labels, with no assigned slots. A deterministic simulation does not generate independent entropy from its fixed initial state and inputs.
