*Register: domestic comedy (Rachel)*

Daniel had labeled the kitchen drawers before either of them knew what went in them. FORKS, the first one said. It held the two forks and a roll of packing tape. UTENSILS (MISC) held a single can opener that Rachel had never seen before and that Daniel claimed had come with the house. The third drawer said LATER.

"What goes in Later?"

"Things we haven't decided about."

She opened it. It was empty. She closed it again.

"That's most things," she said.

He was on the floor with his back against the dishwasher, reading the warranty for the dishwasher. He read warranties as though they might turn on him. Outside, the hill had gone the color of a tennis ball in the last of the afternoon, and below them I-280 went on with its one long sentence.

"Lucien could track the drawers," he said, not looking up. "Inventory."

"Lucien doesn't have hands."

"It doesn't need hands. It needs a camera on the—"

"No."

"I'm just saying it's possible."

"Most things are possible," Rachel said, and put the can opener in Later.
