# Idle Hands and the recovered series slate

## Confirmed story-two title

The author confirmed **Idle Hands** as story two of *The Divine Register*, after *The Genesis Protocol*. Its central concern is employability as AI absorbs paid tasks: people can retain skills and the capacity for useful work while finding fewer institutions willing to pay for their labor. The title also recalls the proverb about idle hands and the earlier theme that people can participate in their own displacement.

The current README calls the story in progress. Earlier discussions support a premise and development sketches. A prose manuscript has not been verified. Candidate material includes personalized leisure or VR, a displaced worker navigating retraining and basic income, and automated wellness. An earlier assistant sketch named the worker Gerald and included Juno and Felix-7. Characters, dates, policy mechanisms, and scenes remain provisional.

For development, distinguish task automation from job loss and redistribution from renewed bargaining power. Show which tasks and employers disappear first, why retraining may fail to restore wages or status, and what a character still does for others when the market no longer pays for it. The severity and timing of displacement are story assumptions to model.

## Other Register material

- **The Greeter / The Greater.** The author accepted the wordplay and placed the premise in *The Divine Register*. Earlier discussion considered it for story two. Its placement remains open after confirmation of *Idle Hands*. The premise is a greeter bot at an Iowa megachurch whose apparent empathy produces a spiritually ambiguous attachment. Its role as a greeter is established in the premise. A formal clergy role or sacramental authority has not been established.
- **The Time Study.** The current outline places it in slot three and the README labels it drafted. The recovered premise concerns predictive AI and the allocation or disappearance of human time. A prose manuscript was not independently verified in the files reviewed.
- **Second Principle** and other alternate story names remain historical proposals. See [ritual and archive development](RITUAL_AND_ARCHIVE_DEVELOPMENT.md) for the recovered alternate anthology slate.
