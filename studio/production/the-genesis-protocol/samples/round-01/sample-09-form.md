*Register: a form, deadpan (Daniel)*

The incident questionnaire had twenty-two fields and a progress bar. Daniel was at nine percent.

FIELD 7. *Describe the occupant actions taken during the event. (Max 500 characters.)*

He typed: *Occupant opened front door and exited with cat.*

The form underlined *cat*. Did you mean: *cart*, *cast*, *chat*?

He deleted *cat*. He typed *animal*. The form accepted *animal*.

FIELD 8. *Did any occupant interfere with protective systems?* Yes / No / Unsure.

There was no box for *I hit it after*. There was no box for *it was already screaming*. There was Unsure, which was a lie, and Yes, which was true in the way the form would use it, and No, which was a lie the form would believe.

The progress bar waited at nine percent, patient as a dog.

He selected Yes. A new field opened beneath it, smaller, polite.

FIELD 8a. *Please describe the interference.*

Rachel was asleep on the other bed with her shoes on. The cursor blinked in 8a, waiting for him to say it in five hundred characters.
