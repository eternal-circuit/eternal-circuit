## Purpose

The recording leaks, clipped. Cerebral's public statement lands, and "payout" is in every headline. The chapter ends at the record's full victory. The backlash appears as specific fragments, never as narrated summary.

## Entry and exit

- **Enters with:** a message from a former colleague asking whether Daniel leaked it.
- **Exits with:** the word *payout* in nearly every headline by morning.

## Turn

It names no amount and quotes no demand. It doesn't need to.
