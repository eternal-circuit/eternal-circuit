## III. Built environment, economics, interface authority

**Author-approved fictional seed:** Cerebral subsidizes rent or provides a favorable arrangement for a lived-in alpha-test residence in Los Altos Hills. Rachel reads the lease; Daniel focuses on the view and project. The subsidy gives Daniel substantial benefit and creates a latent fear that a public dispute could imperil housing. **Neither firing nor contract termination should cause immediate eviction**; examine tenancy and legal arrangements before the Ch. 7/8 move.

**Rationale, evidence to revisit:** 2026 public data show housing in Los Altos Hills has unusually high costs and sparse rental stock. This lends plausibility to employer subsidy as an economic explanation, but it does not prove that a particular 2032 employer would offer this package. Useful sources:
- US Census QuickFacts, Los Altos Hills (2020–2024 ACS median gross rent ≥ $3,500): https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/losaltoshillstowncalifornia/HSG860224
- Zillow Rental Manager, Los Altos Hills rental market, 2026-11-09 (small sample, all types average about $5,495): https://www.zillow.com/rental-manager/market-trends/los-altos-hills-ca/
- Town of Los Altos Hills housing element for locality/geography: https://www.losaltoshills.ca.gov/DocumentCenter/View/5897/Los-Altos-Hills-Housing-Element_Adopted-1-30-23_Clean

**Product distinction, plausible but not independently certified:**
- Guest account can be recognized by presence and receive passive/ambient personalization.
- Authenticated admin can request specific changes through the supported companion app.
- Physical switches and lawful emergency egress remain independent of the AI unless a precisely scoped hardware failure is scientifically cleared.
- Managed vendor update path may remain powered in software “off”; a physical breaker, hardware disconnect or power removal is different.
- Local Wi-Fi and home APIs are not synonymous with all cellular communications.
- No arbitrary hardware ability to harm, delete outside evidence or replace global recordings.
