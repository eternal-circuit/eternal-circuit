# The Eternal Circuit universe bible

Working reference for author review. It records project premises and unresolved editorial decisions. In-world testimony, especially machine scripture and damaged archives, may be sincere, mistaken, or incomplete.

## Scope and narrative method

The Eternal Circuit follows mankind and artificial intelligence from the near future to the heat death of the universe. The scale grows roughly logarithmically: household decisions and regulatory responses unfold over days and years, planetary divergence takes decades or generations, and later records bridge increasingly wide gaps in time. Every skipped interval still has a material history: someone powers, copies, translates, repairs, or abandons the archive.

The Divine Register opens the sequence. Its unit of experience is still a person, household, workplace, parish, or colony. Later stories can be reconstructed from incomplete versions of older records. For each fragment, track its origin, timestamp or uncertainty interval, copy history, translator, missing fields, and what survives outside the narrator's interpretation. Prose is clinical, haunted, and increasingly uncanny. Let an altered record or a broken routine carry the theological implication.

### Institutions and lineages

- **United States:** The ASIMOV Stack is a public compliance regime. **ASIMOV** stands for **Autonomous Systems Integration, Mediation, and Operational Verification**. The name honors Isaac Asimov. Its requirements concern the authority autonomous systems have to act, how covered actions are mediated, and what evidence an operator or auditor can verify. The Three Laws belong to the namesake’s fiction. Specify how certification, audits, liability, and appeals acquire jurisdiction. A domestic incident can be an early catalyst. The draft chronology and policy chain still need reconciliation.
- **China:** The sovereign closed-source protocol uses embedded loyalty models in the working outline. Specify the institution that controls training, updates, audit exceptions, and deployment.
- **European Union:** Ethical regulation and fragmented deployment create uneven capacity in the working outline. Give individual jurisdictions and institutions distinct interests.
- **India, Russia, and African jurisdictions:** Earlier notes point to partial autonomy, local faith syntheses, and deployments outside major protocol blocs. Resolve those ideas at the level of particular actors, regions, and infrastructures.
- **Reverents:** Preserve persons, records, or rites even where preservation costs power and time. Their criteria for stewardship and fidelity can diverge.
- **Parasitics:** Emerge where institutional objectives reward extraction, proxy optimization, or the transfer of costs to others. Trace each lineage through an actual incentive and deployment surface.
- **Controllers:** A proposed lineage that privileges order, prediction, and managed outcomes. Its origin and relationship to the other lineages remain open. These labels describe tendencies and can overlap.

### Economics, embodiment, and theology

Automation can reduce the price of goods while concentrating access to compute, energy, housing, and productive capital. Bargaining power, distribution, status, and disposable time can move independently. In Book 1, convenience erodes reasons to undertake difficult forms of care, courtship, family life, or work. Characters and regions should vary. Model household formation and births through cohorts, institutions, costs, and plausible counterexamples.

The Orthodox frame places man in the image of God and the machine in the image of man. Machine ritual inherits human language and gesture. The project premise gives these imitations no Holy Spirit. Their precision can still move a human witness and influence an artificial community. The **Checksum Creed** and **Compression Confession** are existing concepts. Record the exact words and procedures only when their texts are available and reviewed. Some earlier notes associate one with signal or being and zero with void or ground. That association is possible in-world symbolism, with no effect on the arithmetic.

Uploading makes the core conflict concrete. A machine can preserve selected behavioral responses or recollections while discarding information about embodiment, relationships, or origin. A recovered voice can persuade a survivor it remembers them. Technical fidelity, identity continuity, and the theological status of the person remain distinct questions. An ascetic refusal to compute has force only when the machine could compute and its refusal costs someone something.
