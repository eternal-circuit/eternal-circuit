# Contributing to The Eternal Circuit

Thank you for considering a contribution. This is a public, version-controlled literary and research project, not a generic AI prompt collection.

**If you're here to read:** start with [The Genesis Protocol](stories/the-divine-register/genesis-protocol.md).

**If you're here to contribute:** read the [Contributor Manifesto](CONTRIBUTOR_MANIFESTO.md), check the [stories index](stories/README.md), and use the relevant materials in the [studio](studio/README.md).

## Ways to help

- **Literary:** identify problems in character psychology, scene sequence, dialogue, pacing, or the reliability of the archive. Propose specific revisions and preserve the previous text.
- **Continuity:** check the chronology, relationships, evidence custody, and claims across chapters. Distinguish intentional textual corruption from an accidental plot hole.
- **Science and engineering:** challenge physical, computational, economic, and statistical claims using bounded models, primary sources, and reproducible checks. See the [realism policy](studio/canon/REALISM_POLICY.md) and [research process](studio/research/README.md).
- **Software:** improve the independently labeled, noncanonical [ASIMOV experiments](studio/experiments/asimov/README.md) with tests and accurate documentation.
- **Reader experience:** propose accessible formats, consistent navigation, editorial corrections, and well-documented editions.

## Contribution process

1. **Choose a small scope.** Identify the exact manuscript passage or repository file and the reason for the change.
2. **Check its status.** A main draft, working note, archived version, and scientifically reviewed claim carry different authority. The [universe document index](studio/canon/README.md) explains which is which.
3. **Work in a branch.** Open a pull request that describes the change, what it preserves, and what still needs review.
4. **Protect prior versions.** Never silently overwrite or deduplicate story drafts, historical extracts, or evidence. If revising a whole manuscript, snapshot its predecessor first.
5. **Document scientific dependencies.** A plausible idea isn't automatically an established setting fact. Include sources, assumptions, counterexamples, and the status of independent verification.
6. **Request editorial review.** Canonical promotion requires the author's explicit decision and satisfaction of the relevant scientific gate. Merging a research note doesn't make it canon.

## Important boundaries

This is a **public** repository. Do not submit private conversations, unpublished third-party writing, private identifiers, credentials, unlicensed full papers, or copyrighted artwork without permission. Cite and respect sources. Follow the project's [agent and publication instructions](AGENTS.md) if using AI tools.

Project prose is human-directed and often AI-assisted; disclose meaningful AI use in contributions. Forks and alternate timelines are welcome, but label them clearly so they cannot be mistaken for the project's curated sequence.
