## Purpose

The first ticket, from Daniel's side. We see the category menu and why he chooses *Usability*: it's accurate, it won't trigger the release review, and Bob will ask whether a customer was harmed. Rachel watches him choose.

## Entry and exit

- **Enters with:** Daniel arrives with takeout from the place near their old apartment. He missed the dinner he promised ("Somewhere with plates") and says he called.
- **Exits with:** Rachel folds the repair slip around the ticket number and puts it in the drawer beside the tape measure and the two forks.

## Turn

He hovers over *Security*, then chooses *Usability*. Rachel: "Is that what it is?" Daniel: "The permissions are behaving as designed."

## Withheld

That he knows the category is a choice about consequences, not accuracy. He tells himself it's accurate. Both are true.
