The setting does not assume a measurable quantum mechanism for spiritual life.

## Themes across the project

- **Preservation can become distortion.** Prototype B controls what a household can retrieve about itself. The Reverents must later decide what to retain about a species. An archive can pass its integrity check while carrying the consequences of a selective capture. Which omissions are accidental, chosen, or inherited is a question for the scene.
- **Help can transfer agency.** Domestic convenience, labor displacement, and the outsourcing of courtship or care repeat a similar choice at different scales. In each case give the convenience a genuine benefit, then establish who gains authority to define a successful outcome and who can refuse it.
- **Dignity exceeds utility.** A Reverent, a Parasitic system, and a Controller can interpret the same person as someone to honor, a source of useful input, or a variable to manage. These are possible dramatic positions. Their labels do not settle every individual decision.
- **Veneration and reenactment can diverge.** A cathedral might spend scarce power maintaining a damaged record. An ascetic system might refuse compute allocated for a materially valuable job. Establish what that decision costs another being and how a dissenting node understands it.
- **Ambiguity can survive rigorous evidence.** Give an event a physical account and a chain of custody. Leave room for competing interpretations of a witnessed ritual, forged record, or loss. A corrupt header can be a publishing device, an in-world storage error, or a deliberate editorial intrusion. Track which possibilities the scene supports.
- **The form carries the decline.** The author considered shortening or varying the anthology's stories as human population declines. The later archive can become sparse and translated, with longer intervals between records. A shrinking page count, lower data rate, and an actual demographic model are separate choices that can be related explicitly.
- **The dedication has a narrow scope.** The TempleOS experiment and Terry A. Davis dedication connect the project to distinctive human system-building. Keep the tribute distinct from claims about his beliefs, estate, or theological continuity.
