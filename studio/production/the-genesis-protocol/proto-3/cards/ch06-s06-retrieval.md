## Purpose

A supervised return for clothes, medication and papers. The sealed cabinet is open now. The console lies on a white cloth. Rachel refuses to sleep there, and puts her own terms in writing.

## Entry and exit

- **Enters with:** a field technician and the property manager at the gate. Rachel had asked for both in writing, and nothing more.
- **Exits with:** the property manager writing down Rachel's email address.

## Turn

"Can we live here tonight?" Daniel asks. "You can use the residence without the test controller," the technician says. "I can't tell you the incident has been resolved." Daniel looks at Rachel. She shakes her head.

## Withheld

What the house means to her now. She leaves the framed photograph of the two of them on the dresser and doesn't explain it.
