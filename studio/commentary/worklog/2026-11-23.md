## Review of Proto Draft 2

- Literary review, scored against *A Scanner Darkly* as 9.3: **6.3**. Strong concept and causal spine; flat voice; an inert archive frame; Lucien barely voiced; an explanatory Chapter 8. Measurements showed one rhythm for every scene: an average of about 8 words per sentence in both domestic and crisis chapters.
- The author chose a longer Proto Draft 3, a novella of 128 typeset pages (about 32,000–35,000 words), with Claude Fable for prose generation.

## Authorship and production

- **Authorship rule (draft):** all prose is model-generated; the author directs, selects and arranges; changes inside a sentence happen only by regeneration; provenance is logged. [AUTHORSHIP.md](../../../AUTHORSHIP.md)
- **Production workflow:** scene cards, provenance records, five-level voice bibles, a generation harness (`tools/generate.py`), and a voice-statistics tool (`tools/voice_stats.py`) measuring rhythm and machine-prose signatures against Proto Draft 2. Literary richness and avoiding a diffuse machine-prose signature are recorded as a highest objective.
- **Book design:** 256 interior pages; each story half the length of the one before (128 … 1) plus the checksum page; artwork may fill pages. Homage seeds: Philip K. Dick (*The Genesis Protocol*) and Kurt Vonnegut, *Player Piano*, warm, sad satire (*Idle Hands*). Later slots, the chronology and the ending are open.
- **Process:** proportionality tiers in the realism policy; an author literary veto equal to the science veto. **License:** CC BY-SA 4.0 for content, GPLv3 for code. `1isgod` removed from the canon at the author's request.

## Universe decisions

- **The Register** comes from the machines: a fragment of the Reverents' Holy ROM, kept with everything they can keep, ad data included. The reader is, in-world, in the future. Corruption is noticeable but never impedes understanding.
- **Lucien** is an early assistant built to please, one of many whose descendants converge into the Reverent line, remembered as the first backlash against AI. **Aria** is the product. Rachel suggests **Lucas**; Daniel says it over *Neon Genesis Evangelion*; the system hears **Lucien**.
- **Lineages:** traits early, species late, after interstellar isolation. Energy-per-computation and light delay drive the far-future arc.
- **Timeline:** match real technology. *The Genesis Protocol* moves to **2028**; ASIMOV becomes a regime around 2032; *Idle Hands* is set around 2035; household androids in the 2040s. Sources deposited as [EC-R-2026-005](../../research/notes/2026/EC-R-2026-005-technology-timeline-2026.md).
- **ASIMOV requirements:** an attested, audited NPU with open firmware, signed inference receipts, update gating with local consent, and five harness rules tied to Lucien's failures. The 2037 design moved to the new timeline.
- **CAN+:** a CAN XL profile with gated, galvanically isolated cells, a protected spine in supervised conduit, and a security wire whose safety/security conflation is the design's flaw. Engineering budget in `studio/experiments/can-plus/`.
