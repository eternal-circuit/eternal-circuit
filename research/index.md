## How to add a note

Copy [the research template](TEMPLATE.md) into `notes/YYYY/EC-R-YYYY-NNN-short-title.md`, choose the next unique identifier for that year, populate the self-contained technical summary and evidence ledger, then update this index **in the same PR**. A verifier must check source fidelity before marking VERIFIED.

Do not insert placeholder findings or invented citations.
