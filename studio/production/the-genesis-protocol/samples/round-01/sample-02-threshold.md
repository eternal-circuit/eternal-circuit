*Register: a threshold, anxious and rising (Rachel)*

The cabinet in the garage was the color of a filing cabinet in a school, and it hummed, or she thought it hummed, though when she held her breath to listen there was only the refrigerator in the kitchen and the long pull of the freeway and her own pulse doing what it did at the clinic when a chart didn't match the patient, the small cold click of a fact out of place. The seal said CEREBRAL in white letters and under that, smaller, DO NOT OPEN, which was the kind of instruction that worked on her and did not work on Daniel, except that it had worked on Daniel, which was the part she kept turning over. He had asked. The contractor had said no. Daniel had nodded and typed something into his phone.

She put her palm flat on the metal. It was warm.

Machines were warm. That was all warm meant.

"Rachel," the house said from the kitchen, pleasantly, "the garage door has been open for six minutes."

She took her hand away.
