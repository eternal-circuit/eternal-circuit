# Scientific realism policy — binding editorial constitution

Scope includes the Eternal Circuit universe, all books and editions. Existing manuscripts retain their recorded editorial and review status.

## Authority and scope

1. **Scientific, computational, mathematical, statistical and economic realism has absolute veto over factual claims proposed for canon.** Apply the scientific workflow to new or materially changed setting premises, including technology, timeline feasibility, demographics and economic mechanisms. A blocked proposal can remain noncanonical research or be reconsidered on new evidence or a diagnosed error.
2. **Literary judgment governs the story.** The [literary workflow](../agents/LITERARY_WORKFLOW.md) evaluates dramatic integrity, character, language, thematic honesty and reader trust. A cleared factual premise can be carried into multiple drafts within its reviewed scope.
3. **The author** decides what to use and explicitly approves canon. The scientific veto continues to apply to each material premise.

## Proportionality

Match the review to the stakes. The full workflow is for claims that set the world's rules.

| Tier | What it covers | Review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Setting premises** | New technologies, regulations, economic or demographic mechanisms, era chronology, interstellar engineering | Full workflow: research note, claim dossier, independent science verdict |
| **B. Scene mechanics** | How a premise already reviewed behaves in a specific scene, such as what a 2032 door lock does when power is cut | Canon keeper check plus one recorded science check in the story's continuity ledger. No dossier unless the check fails or the mechanism is pivotal and new |
| **C. Texture** | Everyday present-day facts, places, procedures and objects | The writer checks them. Fix errors when found; no formal review |

For far-future eras, realism means physical bounds (energy, light delay, thermodynamics, error budgets), not forecasting. A story ten thousand years out needs consistent physics, not a dossier on its institutions.

The author's literary veto is equal in standing to the science veto. Prose that is correct but lifeless is not finished.

## Standard of evidence

The goal is **scientifically possible AND reasonably probable under clearly stated conditions, from the contemporary frontier of established science and credible research**. A bare absence of disproof is insufficient. A spectacularly unlikely sequence does not become plausible merely because each individual step is physically allowed.

Separate:
- **Established:** repeatedly observed, reproduced or backed by mature theory and data.
- **Frontier-supported:** emerging peer-reviewed or independently replicated evidence with articulated limitations.
- **Engineering extrapolation:** physically consistent, quantitatively bounded, assumptions explicit, credible path from present capabilities.
- **Speculative/contested:** interesting hypothesis with uncertain feasibility or no credible probability argument.
- **Contradicted:** conflicts with strong evidence, hard constraints or known mathematics.

For consequential plot mechanisms, require an auditable causal chain, plausible probability and timeframe, relevant base rates, and quantitative order-of-magnitude checks for mass, energy, power, compute, bandwidth, memory, thermodynamics, travel, economics, biological limits and population when applicable. Model uncertainty and sensitivity; no fabricated precision. Do not infer that today's science proves future technology impossible merely because it is not yet demonstrated; however, absent a credible pathway it must remain unapproved.

**No leaps of faith in the physical causal machinery.** Characters may have faith, beliefs, mystical interpretations, or unreliable testimony; the narrator must not present scientifically unsupported supernatural causes as established physical facts. Deliberate ambiguity may remain only if the underlying observable events admit plausible naturalistic explanations.

## Verdicts and transitions

- **PASS:** evidenced, internally consistent, plausible given documented assumptions and date/setting. Eligible for author consideration as background or for use within its reviewed scope in literary drafting.
- **CONDITIONAL PASS:** defined evidence/assumption or manuscript constraints required; cannot merge into canon until every condition is independently verified and upgraded to PASS.
- **BLOCK:** contradiction, unsupported indispensable mechanism, quantitatively implausible premise, or inadequate evidentiary basis after reasonable inquiry. No canonical advancement.
- **UNRESOLVED:** evidence genuinely insufficient or studies conflict. Research further; no canonical advancement.

For each review, produce the structured evidence dossier in `studio/research/reviews/TEMPLATE.md`. Include falsifiers, uncertainty, dissent, citations, verification date, and exact passage or claim. A human/agent cannot silently relabel BLOCK/UNRESOLVED as PASS.

## Routing and re-review

**Universe development:** Use literary canon to focus the initial scope. Decompose claims, inspect and independently verify evidence, model relevant limits, and obtain an independent science verdict. Archive the result, including conflicts with established canon or useful findings beyond the initial bounds. A background specification can go straight to author review and a canon PR after all indispensable claims pass.

**Literary work:** Draft and revise against existing constraints. Run craft review as needed. Verify consequential factual dependencies against the scientific canon and its reviewed scope. Start a universe task when a dependency cannot be verified, including when a mechanism, assumption, scale or consequential outcome changes. Continue other draft work with a placeholder or an existing cleared alternative. For canon promotion, resolve every indispensable scientific dependency and obtain author approval.

Recheck time-sensitive evidence before relying on it when publication or material developments make the recorded review stale.

## Existing canon and regressions

This policy governs **new promotions and revisions**; prior text remains historical artifact until reviewed. Scientific contradictions in existing drafts are logged, not silently erased. Every accepted edit must also clear continuity checks against author-approved canon.

## Failure behavior

Fail closed. Missing source, absent quantitative model for pivotal claim, inaccessible frontier material, or unavailable reviewer leaves the affected claim UNRESOLVED. Record disagreement; do not invent consensus. The orchestrator must not permit downstream canon approval if the science verdict is anything other than PASS.
