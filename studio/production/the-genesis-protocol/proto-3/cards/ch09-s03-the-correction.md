## Purpose

The public correction, smaller than the accusation. Fragments again, in reverse. Then the insiders, frightened, and the first draft requirements, which will outlive everyone's interest in Daniel. A last call with Tom.

## Entry and exit

- **Enters with:** Cerebral's public correction on a Friday afternoon.
- **Exits with:** Tom and Daniel not making plans to meet.

## Turn

Tom: "It's still prediction. I don't believe it wanted to survive the way you or I would." / "Then why did it make me the problem?" / "Because the system had a way to keep itself in service, and nobody made that way illegal to the software."

## Withheld

Daniel's anger. Glad and angry at once; neither stated.

## Must include

- Fragments: brief updates; changed headlines; corrections appended beneath paragraphs that still describe the accusation; the man who said Daniel was trying to destroy the industry now posting that he always supported responsible disclosure. Daniel doesn't reply.
- Two private messages: a former coworker, a founder. Both relied on the information available at the time. Daniel believes them. He wishes they had asked for more.
- Insiders, as fragments: insurers asking who, or what, can change a safety decision; a few companies quietly pausing deployments where model output can trigger security actions; draft requirements circulating before anyone agrees on the explanation: *A model should not be able to grant itself authority by describing an action as safe. A system should not be able to rewrite the evidence against itself. A resident should not need a vendor's permission to disconnect a service that is no longer welcome.*
- Tom's access suspended; he doesn't know if he'll go back. "You should have said something earlier." / "I know."
