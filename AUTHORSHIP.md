# Authorship rule

**Status:** DRAFT for author approval. Proposed 2026-11-23. Applies to manuscripts begun after approval, starting with *The Genesis Protocol*, Proto Draft 3. Earlier drafts keep the provenance recorded for them in [`studio/canon/PROJECT_INVENTORY.md`](studio/canon/PROJECT_INVENTORY.md).

*The Eternal Circuit* is an experiment in directed AI authorship. All prose is machine-generated. A human author directs it, selects it and is accountable for it.

## The rule

1. **Every word of published prose is generated by a model.** This covers narration, dialogue, in-world documents and archive metadata that appear in the reader-facing text.
2. **The human author directs.** The author writes scene cards, briefs, revision notes, constraints and voice references, and decides what each scene must do, withhold and avoid.
3. **The human author selects.** For each scene the author chooses among generated variants, or rejects all of them and asks for more.
4. **The human author arranges.** The author may delete text, reorder it, and join generated passages at sentence boundaries.
5. **The human author does not type surviving prose.** Any change inside a sentence, including a single word, is made by regeneration or a model edit made under instruction, never by hand. A model also makes spelling and punctuation corrections, under instruction.
6. **Provenance is recorded.** Each scene logs the model, the instructions and inputs, the variants generated, and the author's decision. See [`studio/production/PROVENANCE.md`](studio/production/PROVENANCE.md).

## What counts as direction

These are human work. They are not prose, and they may be published as part of the project's record:

- scene cards, beat sheets, outlines and story briefs
- revision notes ("colder," "end one beat earlier," "she never says this aloud")
- the author's own experience given as material for a scene
- voice references: the author's own writing, or generated passages the author has selected as targets
- canon, research and continuity documents

Passages from other published writers may guide style privately. Do not commit them to this public repository. Describe the quality you want from them instead.

## Open decisions

- **Titles and headings.** Proposed: story, chapter and series titles may be human-authored. Body text and archive metadata are generated.
- **Front matter.** Proposed: dedications and author's notes outside the fiction may be human-authored and should be labeled as such.

## Why

The project's central question is what persists when human work is delegated to machines. Its own method answers part of that question in public: the machine writes every sentence, and a person decides which sentences exist. The provenance record lets readers see the difference.
