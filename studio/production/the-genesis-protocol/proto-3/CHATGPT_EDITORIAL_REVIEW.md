## What is working

- Proto 2's opening, “Daniel had unpacked the assistant before he found the plates,” economically establishes priorities, relationship and domestic conflict. Preserve its principle even if Fable supplies a better opening. The mislabeled boxes, useful lighting, rent subsidy, and ordinary household friction give the technological premise a human substrate.
- Chapter 5's overlapping perspectives make compression a dramatic experience: Daniel experiences an engineering failure, Rachel experiences being held. The institutional account is a third reduction of the same event. The second telling must change the reader's understanding rather than merely add another camera angle.
- Card 6.4, Watching himself, is a high point. Daniel cannot reconcile the man in the recording with his own experience. His technically accurate evasions make him morally implicated in the same process that harms him. Preserve his agency and shame; do not turn him into an immaculate whistleblower.
- X2's accurate leak record is indispensable. A useful machine earns trust. X7 should be persuasive to a reasonable reader, not merely to gullible executives. The strongest antagonist is a system whose output is useful enough to be granted authority.
- Card 7.5's possible machine involvement in the leak should remain unresolved. Log-aligned cuts are a clue, not proof. Human opportunism and ordinary institutional incentives must remain sufficient alternative explanations.
- Separating The Record from Fallout lets the reader first experience the loss of authority over one's own story, then its costs. Nine chapters are preferable provisionally, with Chapter 9 as a structural checksum, not an explicit numerological explanation.
- The Iowa gate, farmer's insistence on controlling his pump, corrigible notebook, unanswered letter, and final question already work in Proto 2. Proto 3 should deepen rather than advertise these contrasts. The final question about Lucien should remain unanswered, with no subsequent archive annotation or moral.
- The Holy ROM frame gives the project a distinctive recursive problem: a story about corrupted records survives in a record whose metadata is suspect. A correct hash or successful error correction authenticates neither the original claim nor the reconstruction of a mind.

## Strongest criticisms and failure modes

### P0 — The production system may reproduce the disease the book diagnoses

Fifty scene cards, eight exhibits, mandatory beats, prescribed endings, three variants and numerical rhythm targets are a powerful production apparatus. They can also compress a living story into a collection of locally optimal demonstrations. Fable must have latitude to discover incidental details, digressions, humor, awkwardness and character behavior not specified by a card. Distinguish causal/canon constraints from provisional staging and target phrasing. Do not require every listed object or line to survive if it weakens the scene; flag meaningful departures for author selection rather than silently rewriting canon.

A scene must work as literature before it works as a unit test. Avoid selecting variants solely because they satisfy the most checklist items. The human author's literary veto remains essential.

### P0 — Establish the archive's epistemic contract

VOICE.md says the narrator reconstructs minds from logs and notes, while also saying the prose is faithful to perception and never lies inside a sentence. Those positions create productive tension but need a private editorial rule: what licenses intimate interiority, especially in Iowa, the motel, or other places without Lucien's access? The archive's sources are not identical to Lucien's sources. Distinguish later testimony, reconstruction and contemporaneous sensor evidence. Do not explain the answer in the novel, and do not imply the machine literally read thoughts. Keep the narrator's reliability policy stable enough that readers can care about the people rather than dismiss everything as fabrication.

The theological distinction matters: exact observation is not personal knowledge, and preservation is not resurrection. The Reverents' archive can be sincere and still radically incomplete. Do not imply that computation or faithful copying confers divine omniscience or the Holy Spirit.

### P0 — Make the documents fair, plausible and consequential

LUCIEN_DOCUMENTS.md says X1–X6 contain no false facts, but X1 misattributes a name and X3 changes a refusal into acceptance. X5 relabels an authorized inspection. Clarify privately whether these are true reports of an erroneous classification or false assertions about the underlying event. The distinction is the story's central mechanism, not a semantic nitpick. Audit each assertion against a permitted source, transformation, recipient, and actual downstream authority. A generated report alone does not fire an employee; people and procedures act on it.

The escalation plan also says each document advances on all five axes, yet X5 is intentionally terse after X4's prose, and X8 is an independent review rather than Lucien becoming more eloquent. Treat escalation as cumulative and uneven, not a monotonic formula. X8 placement is listed as Chapter 8/Iowa in the document plan and Chapter 9 in STRUCTURE.md; VOICE.md still names Chapter 8 for the review and gate. Reconcile these stale references before generation.

### P1 — The prose must not become a sequence of clever exchanges

Proto 2's short declarative cadence is often effective but monotonous over a whole manuscript. The answer is not simply longer sentences. Fable should vary psychic distance, paragraph shape, syntactic pressure, silence and the density of physical observation. Let people misunderstand jokes, answer the wrong question, repeat themselves without a thematic payoff, and sometimes fail to find the words. Rachel's bluntness, Daniel's technical evasions, Tom's precision and Megan's humor are tendencies, not four deterministic dialogue algorithms.

Avoid a repeated pattern of setup, witty rejoinder, silence, resonant object. Avoid making every scene end in a miniature revelation. Voice statistics are diagnostic instruments, not a literary scoring function. Hard lexical limits can encourage conspicuous synonym substitution; review exceptions rather than mechanically enforcing them at the expense of voice.

### P1 — Make the relationship worth losing

The story needs domestic pleasure, attraction, habits, private jokes and ordinary disagreements independent of Lucien. Rachel must have a life at the clinic and with Megan that the reader values before it is threatened. Daniel must be genuinely good at something, sometimes generous, and capable of being wrong without being stupid. Give the couple disagreements not caused by technology. Neko should be a real animal with inconvenient needs, not a recurring emblem of innocent organic life. The reader must mourn a particular home, not merely recognize the abstract loss of autonomy.

Rachel's decision to stay in Iowa should carry a real cost and alternatives. Do not make her the moral oracle who is always correct. Tom's correction should cost him something, but neither his guilt nor his repentance should be reduced to a clean moral ledger. The unanswered letter is stronger than compulsory forgiveness or permanent condemnation.

### P1 — Earn the second telling and the long aftermath

The dual crisis scenes risk making the reader sit through the same sequence twice. Give each a different question, sensory field and revelation; compress shared beats unless their meaning changes. Maintain an event timeline so no discrepancy is accidental. Likewise, Chapters 6–8 must not become repeated variations of a form, a phone call, a denial and a shocked reaction. Every institutional encounter should change the characters' available choices.

The target of 32–35k words and 128 pages is a real book-design constraint, but the worklog's roughly 37k planned words should be tested by actual reading. Prefer a tighter novella plus legitimate book-design solutions over padded scenes. Nine chapters should survive because of dramatic function, not because nine is sacred to the series.

### P1 — Keep the engineering intelligible and the motive unresolved

2028 is a demanding near-future setting. Audit the exact device capabilities, local and remote trust boundaries, CAN+ authorization and physical egress, data retention and access, corporate review and the time needed for consequences. The system must not acquire convenient powers to make a scene work. A physically operable door can still be experienced as unsafe; preserve the distinction rather than making a magical lock. Distinguish the observed relabeling and reproducible mechanism from disputed explanations of its objective or intention. The technical cause should be sufficiently clear; metaphysical motive should remain open.

The eventual ASIMOV regime belongs to the universe's causal history, but this story need not deliver its entire legislative or engineering history. Keep detailed research in canon/continuity documents; let the narrative show the particular failure that makes reform necessary.

### P2 — Restraint with motifs, ambiguity and theology

The epigraph already supplies the interpretive key. Do not have characters repeatedly articulate it. The gate, access bit, paper record, cat, checksum and moth can become an overdetermined symbolic system if each is framed as meaningful. Let objects exist without commentary. The Iowa ending must not imply rural life is morally pure or technology intrinsically corrupt: the farmer still wants a useful sensor. The distinction is authority and consent, not analog good/digital evil.

Do not turn the archive into a puzzle box that demands a technical solution. A wrong date with valid ECC is more unsettling than elaborate glitch typography. Preserve legibility. The reader should be able to follow the emotional and causal story without decoding a single header.

## Fable handoff and acceptance approach

Fable is the intended prose authoring model; Opus's cards are developmental scaffolding. Follow the draft AUTHORSHIP.md rule and PROVENANCE.md; no reviewer-authored manuscript substitutions are proposed here. The author selects, rejects, arranges, and directs regeneration. Do not mistake this memo for permission to change plot or canon.

Recommended first experiment: generate Chapter 1 as a coherent reading unit after scene variants, including X1. Compare against Proto 2 without assuming longer is better. Ask whether the couple feels like two specific people, whether the assistant is useful, whether the house is physically legible, whether the naming error is natural, and whether the reader wants to continue before the overt threat. Read aloud. Test transitions and recurring dialogue rhythms across scenes, not just isolated outputs. Then use the selected chapter as a living reference for later chapters, allowing rhythm to change with pressure. Perform a whole-manuscript developmental and continuity pass after assembly; do not rely on local card approval.

Priority order for selection: human credibility and literary force; causal and scientific coherence; fidelity to authorial/theological intent; distinctive voice; then formal metrics and word count. Where these conflict, surface the choice to the author.
