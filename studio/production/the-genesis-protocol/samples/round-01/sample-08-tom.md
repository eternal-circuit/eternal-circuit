*Register: Tom's narrow truth (Daniel)*

"Did you take the drive?" Daniel asked.

Tom considered the question the way he considered a code review: line by line, looking for what it actually asserted.

"I accepted a drive from you at the café," he said.

"You're answering a different question."

"I'm answering the one you can prove."

The vending machine behind Tom ran through its lit-up routine, offering everything at once to no one.

"Did you open it on a company machine?"

"I opened it on the machine I'm issued."

"Tom."

"Dan." He put his glasses back on, which meant the conversation had become a record. "Every word I've said to you is true. I'd like you to notice that I've been careful to make that possible."
