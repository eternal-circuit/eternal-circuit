# The Genesis Protocol — setting bible

**Status:** DRAFT, 2026-11-24, from the author's memories of living in Los Altos Hills and the geography of Proto Draft 0. Capture the *type* of house and neighborhood. **Privacy rule:** no street names, house numbers or photographs of real residences in this public repository or in the manuscript.

## The neighborhood

- Hillside lots in Los Altos Hills above I-280, large and irregular, divided by hedges more than fences.
- Narrow asphalt lanes with no sidewalks, edges littered with eucalyptus bark strips and leaves. Driveways drop or climb steeply from the road.
- Black wrought-iron gates; a white lamp post with lantern globes at the end of a driveway; cracked asphalt patched with gravel.
- Oleander hedges (pink flowers, narrow leaves, poisonous; nobody in the story needs to say so), tall eucalyptus with peeling bark, the odd palm, purple-leaf ornamental trees.
- Behind the houses, open grass hills with scattered oaks along the ridge.
- **Sightlines (from Proto Draft 0, keep):** I-280 below as a low, continuous band of motion and sound. The Stanford Dish on the opposite hills.

## The house

- A single-story ranch from the 1960s or 70s. Low-pitched roof of flat brown tile with wide eaves; cream board siding; a brick chimney with metal caps; skylights.
- **An old TV antenna is still on the roof**, unused for decades. Daniel likes it.
- A big open living room with wide windows facing the slope.
- **A raised back deck with orange clay tiles**, and orange and lemon trees. This is Rachel's place, the "patio" of earlier drafts.
- Old bones, new nerves: the original structure retrofitted with CAN+. The garage holds a gray Cerebral cabinet with a tamper seal where the spine terminates.
- A kitchen window looking out over the slope (Proto Draft 2).

## Senses

| Sense | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Sound | Wind somewhere off in the distance; I-280's steady hum; coyotes now and then at night; the old house ticking in its walls when the heat comes on; the console's small fan |
| Smell | Eucalyptus and sage; **citrus blossom** from the deck in April; warm wood |
| Light | Golden afternoon light across the living room; haze on the horizon; long shadows over the hills at dusk |
| Season | **April: the hills are green** from winter rain. By the end of April they begin to turn. By June the story is in Iowa and the corn is just coming up. The landscape keeps time without comment. |

**Wildfire smoke:** the author remembers the smoke from wildfires. April is outside fire season, so it appears as aftermath, not weather. The air purifiers still carry a "smoke day" routine from last fall, Mrs. Thompson mentions the smoke, and there is ash in a gutter Daniel hasn't cleaned. It's a reminder that the house has already been managed through one emergency.

## Motifs in the landscape

- **Listening at every scale:** the dead antenna on the roof, the console on the counter, the Dish across the valley. The text never draws the connection.
- **The view looks finished.** From the deck the house looks complete while the inside is still in boxes (Proto Draft 2, Chapter 1).
- **Gates:** the wrought-iron gate at the street, the farm gate in Iowa, and CAN+'s gates. Use sparingly.

## Words to avoid

From Proto Draft 0's habits: *verdant*, *lush*, *tapestry*, *iconic*, *haven*, *tranquility*, *soothing backdrop*, *nestled*, *panoramic*, *breathtaking*. Name the thing (oleander, eucalyptus bark, the Dish) instead of praising it.
